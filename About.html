Lona is a location-based marketplace platform that connects Filipino artists with local customers across the Philippines and also acts as an online portfolio, and social media. The platform empowers artists to showcase their work, offer services, and build sustainable creative careers while enabling customers to discover and commission talented artists in their communities.

What Lona Means

  • Canvas (where art comes to life)
  • Community (local, intimate, connected)
  • Simplicity (easy to remember and say)

Mission

To build thriving local artist communities across the Philippines by making art accessible, artists discoverable, and creative services trusted through technology that prioritizes connection over transaction.

For Artists

Lona provides a digital portfolio and storefront where artists can:

  • Showcase their work and creative services
  • Set their own prices and service offerings
  • Build credibility through verified reviews and badges
  • Connect with fellow artists and build professional networks

For Customers

Lona offers a curated discovery platform where customers can:

  • Find talented local artists by location and specialty
  • Browse portfolios and read verified reviews
  • Commission custom work or purchase ready-made services
  • Communicate directly with artists
  • Track orders and deliveries in real-time
